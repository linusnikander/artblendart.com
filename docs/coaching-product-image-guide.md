# Shopify Product Image Specifications for ArtBlendArt Coaching

## Technical Requirements

| Specification | Value | Notes |
|---------------|-------|-------|
| **Primary Size** | 2048 x 2048 px | Square 1:1 ratio (Shopify standard) |
| **Minimum Size** | 1024 x 1024 px | For acceptable quality |
| **File Format** | PNG or JPEG | PNG for transparency, JPEG for photos |
| **Color Space** | sRGB | Web-optimized |
| **Max File Size** | 20 MB | Keep under 5 MB for fast loading |

## Image Prompt Templates

### 1. "Din kreativa plats" (Your Creative Place)

*Theme: Finding creative joy, exploration, beginnings*

```
Prompt for AI Image Generator:

Create a soft, aspirational product image for a creative coaching service.

Scene: A serene, light-filled workspace or nature setting showing creative exploration. Include subtle elements like art supplies (brushes, sketchbook), soft natural light, plants, or an outdoor creative moment.

Style: Modern Scandinavian minimalism with soft purple (#7c3aed) and lavender (#f8f5ff) color accents. Warm, inviting, and calming atmosphere. Soft depth of field with dreamy bokeh.

Mood: Hopeful beginnings, quiet inspiration, gentle creativity without pressure.

Composition: Clean and uncluttered with breathing room. Subject slightly off-center. Soft shadows. No text or logos.

Lighting: Golden hour warmth or soft diffused daylight.

Format: Square 1:1 ratio, 2048x2048 pixels, suitable for e-commerce.
```

---

### 2. "Ide till verklighet" (Idea to Reality)

*Theme: Transformation, momentum, making it happen*

```
Prompt for AI Image Generator:

Create a dynamic yet elegant product image for a creative coaching transformation program.

Scene: Visual metaphor for creative transformation - hands shaping clay, a sketch becoming a finished piece, seeds sprouting, or a path leading forward through beautiful landscape.

Style: Modern Scandinavian aesthetic with purple gradient tones (#7c3aed to #6d28d9) woven into the composition. Clean, professional, but warm.

Mood: Empowering momentum, confident progress, creative breakthrough. Energetic but grounded.

Composition: Dynamic diagonal lines or flowing movement. Focal point clearly defined. Clean negative space. No text.

Lighting: Bright, optimistic lighting with soft purple/violet color cast.

Format: Square 1:1 ratio, 2048x2048 pixels, suitable for e-commerce.
```

---

### 3. "Kickstart" (Wellness/Friskvardspaket)

*Theme: Fresh start, balance, wellness, single session energy*

```
Prompt for AI Image Generator:

Create a fresh, wellness-focused product image for a creative coaching kickstart session.

Scene: Balance between creativity and wellbeing - morning light through window onto art supplies, person in peaceful creative moment outdoors, or zen-like creative workspace with plants.

Style: Scandinavian wellness aesthetic. Combine soft purple (#7c3aed) with natural green (#059669) accents. Light, airy, and refreshing.

Mood: Fresh energy, new beginnings, healthy balance, accessible creativity. Feels like taking a deep breath.

Composition: Balanced, symmetrical or rule-of-thirds. Lots of white/light space. Natural elements (wood, plants, natural light). No text.

Lighting: Bright morning light, fresh and clean. Soft highlights.

Format: Square 1:1 ratio, 2048x2048 pixels, suitable for e-commerce.
```

## Additional AI Prompt Modifiers

**Add these to any prompt for brand consistency:**

```
Additional style notes:
- Scandinavian minimalism with warm, human touch
- Color temperature: Slightly warm (not cold/clinical)
- Include subtle purple/violet tones (#7c3aed) in lighting, shadows, or accents
- Photography style: Editorial lifestyle, not stock photo generic
- Avoid: Cluttered compositions, harsh shadows, overly saturated colors, cliche "lightbulb" creativity symbols
- Target audience: Swedish women 35-55 seeking meaningful creative pursuits
- Quality: Professional product photography, suitable for premium pricing
```
