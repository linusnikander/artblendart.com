How does it work?

My coaching consists of conversations, and my experience is that most people need at least three sessions to get an accurate picture of what the coaching can offer and feel comfortable in the process. The sessions are 60 minutes long.

The first session

We explore your idea, your direction and what stands in the way of you moving forward. You get help clarifying what you want to create and begin translating it into concrete actions – both artistic and practical. Already here we set something in motion.

After each session you receive a written summary and inspiring, action-based homework – tailored to you, your project and your material choices – that help you continue working between our meetings. We end each session with a clear direction for the next step.

All material is sent to you, so that you have your process collected – a living document of your creation that you can return to.

You don't need to prepare for the first session, but if you reflect on your idea and what you long to create, we can use the time even more effectively.
