How does it work?

My coaching consists of conversations, and my experience is that most people need at least three sessions to get an accurate picture of what the coaching can offer and feel comfortable in the process. The sessions are 60 minutes long.

The first session

Explores your current situation, your longings and how I can support you going forward. You get help formulating goals, gaining new perspectives on your creation and already here tools to get started.

After each session you receive a written summary and inspiring homework, practical, creative or reflective, that keeps your work developing between meetings. We end each session with a brief summary and a plan for what you want to focus on until next time.

All material is sent to you, so that you have your process documented, a personal tool you can return to whenever you want.

You don't need to prepare for the first session, but if you reflect a little on what you want to get out of the coaching before we meet, we can use the time even more effectively.
